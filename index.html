ShareMeList

Shared grocery lists & bill splitting

  • iOS 17+
  • SwiftUI
  • Swift 6
  • Supabase
  • Realtime

A shopping list you share with the people you live with. Add an item on one phone and it shows up on everyone's straight away, even after a stretch offline in the shop. After shopping, the app splits the bill and keeps track of who owes whom.

What it does

  • Shared lists that sync live between everyone on them
  • Works fully offline and catches up when the connection returns
  • Quick entry: “2 kg apples” fills in the quantity for you
  • Groups products by aisle automatically, in English and Polish
  • Invite by QR code, link or AirDrop
  • Checkout splits each trip to the cent, in multiple currencies, with balances and history

How it's built

  • Offline-first sync engine. The UI is a server-confirmed snapshot with a persistent outbox of pending changes on top, so updates feel instant and survive restarts
  • Conflict handling. Edits are per-field last-write-wins, client-generated IDs make retries idempotent, and versioned tombstones mean deleted items never come back
  • Security on the backend. Postgres row-level security decides every read and write. Invitations and disconnects are atomic SQL functions with their own SQL test suite
  • Clean layering. A SwiftUI app sits on a local Swift package. Its dependency-free core runs against a BackendService protocol and is unit-tested with an in-memory backend
  • Localization. Full English and Polish, including Polish plural forms, gender-neutral wording and switching language in the app without a restart

CSDeveloper

iOS interview prep with on-device AI

  • iOS 26
  • SwiftUI
  • SwiftData
  • Foundation Models
  • SPM

A study app for iOS developer interviews. Curated questions with clear answers, key points and common pitfalls, plus spaced-repetition review and quizzes. An AI study assistant runs on device with Apple Intelligence.

What it does

  • Curated iOS interview Q&A with key points, pitfalls and follow-up questions
  • Spaced-repetition review that schedules each card for the right time
  • Quizzes, including questions generated by AI
  • “Ask AI” chat about any topic
  • Works offline. No account, ads or tracking

How it's built

  • Modular architecture. 10 local Swift packages: domain, persistence, content, design system and AI, plus one package per feature
  • On-device AI. Apple's Foundation Models framework powers quiz generation and chat. A provider abstraction can switch to OpenAI with the user's own key, stored in the Keychain
  • Spaced repetition. An SM-2 scheduler lives in the pure domain layer, with progress stored in SwiftData
  • Content pipeline. Versioned content packs come from a manifest on GitHub Pages. The app checks ETags, verifies each pack's SHA-256 checksum and keeps working offline
  • Testing. Unit tests use Swift Testing, and an XCUITest suite drives the app to produce the App Store screenshots
